How BookWise recommends books

BookWise uses a hybrid recommender. Three simple ideas are combined into one score for every book you haven't read:

  1. Content-based (45%) – every book is turned into a numeric "fingerprint" (TF-IDF of its genres, author, title and description). We build a taste profile from the books you liked and the genres you chose, then find books with similar fingerprints (cosine similarity).
  2. Collaborative (40%) – "readers who liked this also liked that". Books that were rated similarly by the same people are considered similar (item-based collaborative filtering).
  3. Popularity (15%) – a fair, weighted average rating that helps when we don't know you yet.

The weights adapt: with few ratings we lean on your chosen genres and popularity; as you rate more books, collaborative filtering gets a bigger say.

What counts as feedback?

  • ⭐ Ratings (5★ = loved it, 1★ = disliked – low ratings push similar books down)
  • ♥ Favourites (treated as strong positive signals)
  • 👀 Books you view (a weak positive signal)

Every recommendation comes with a short reason so you can see what drove it.
